<h2 data-importer="text" align="left">About me</h2>

<p data-importer="text" align="left">I build systems that reason, decide, and act. I like to start from the fundamentals — automata, graphs, concurrency, networks — and build upward from there, toward autonomous agents that can make decisions on their own. I'm especially drawn to the intersection of symbolic AI, reinforcement learning, and concurrent programming, and how those ideas turn into systems that actually work, not just diagrams on paper. I'd rather understand how something works before leaning on whatever abstraction is trendy, so I try to build things from scratch at least once. JARVIS, my personal assistant, is where it all comes together — the place where theory becomes something I actually use, and that keeps evolving as I learn.</p>
